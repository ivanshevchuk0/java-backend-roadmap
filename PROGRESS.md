## AGH brief and deadline board

Use [open AGH issues](https://github.com/ivanshevchuk0/java-backend-roadmap/issues?q=is%3Aissue+is%3Aopen+label%3A%22track%3Aagh%22). Create an **AGH assignment** issue for each real assignment with a distinct deadline. Record subject/exercise/current lecture, authoritative rubric, exact due time/time zone, required tools, remaining hours, submission format, checks, and receipt.

Unknown dates stay unknown until verified. Implemented, tested, and submitted are different states. Local folders are pointers, not grading evidence. Spring does not substitute for Flask assignments.
